Billed − Cost = Profit

Billed is what we charge the account; cost is what we paid the officer (or partner). Margin is profit ÷ billed. Figures snapshot on completion, so scheduled-but-undone work doesn't inflate today's earnings.

Customers and partners together

Partner accounts (Nexus, Keyholding Co) appear alongside direct customers — partner-app jobs still carry an officer cost we track for pay, even when no client report is produced.

Recurring vs one-off

Annual recurring revenue (ARR) comes from keyholding subscriptions; the rest is per-activity (alarms, patrols, lock-ups, VPIs, ad-hoc). Setup fees are billed once at onboarding.
